About Sea Lions

Sea lions are intelligent marine mammals known for their loud barks, playful nature, and incredible swimming abilities. Unlike seals, sea lions have visible ear flaps and can walk on land using their large flippers.

They are highly social animals that live in colonies and are found along the coasts of the Pacific Ocean.

Where They Live

Coastal Waters

From California to the Galápagos Islands and along the Pacific coast.

Rocky Shores

They haul out on rocky beaches and docks to rest and sunbathe.

Deep Divers

Can dive up to 600 feet (180 meters) deep in search of food.

Protect Sea Lions

Sea lions face threats from plastic pollution, overfishing, and climate change. You can help by reducing single-use plastics and supporting marine conservation organizations.
